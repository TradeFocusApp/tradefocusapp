# TradeFocus

Turn your watchlist into a clear trade plan so you can focus on execution.

No manual chart reading. No guesswork.

Works for both swing and intraday trading.

## 🚀 Try it

https://tradefocus.streamlit.app/

![TradeFocus Screenshot](tradefocusapp.png)

## Why TradeFocus

Most traders spend more time figuring out what to trade than actually trading.

TradeFocus helps you skip that and go straight to execution.

## What it does

- Converts your watchlist into a structured Trade Plan  
- Ranks setups by strength and priority  
- Provides position sizing and risk/reward levels  
- Helps avoid overtrading by focusing on the best setups
